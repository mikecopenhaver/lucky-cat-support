Privacy Policy

Lucky Cat Gold Fortunes · Effective November 12, 2026

Lucky Cat Gold Fortunes ("the app") is made by Michael Copenhaver. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

The app has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for or store your name, email, phone number or location. Your fortunes are generated on your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad interaction data and crash or performance diagnostics. On iOS, you can choose whether to allow tracking when prompted, and you can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized with your advertising identifier.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
